Prepare your setup ahead of time

Use the official College Board instructions for your test administration to check identification, admission information, permitted items, and device readiness. Open Bluebook ahead of time and complete the required setup. Requirements can change, so use the official checklist rather than treating this guide as an admission policy.

Practice with the tools you will use

The digital SAT uses Bluebook and includes a built-in Desmos calculator for Math. Rehearse with the calculator, answer elimination, and review tools during official practice. Learn how to mark a question and return to it within the current module.

Use a repeatable pacing decision

When stuck, identify the missing step. If you still have no useful next move, choose your best answer, mark the question, and continue. Return while the current module remains open. Do not plan to return to an earlier module after its time ends.

Review with purpose

Check unanswered questions first. Next, revisit marked questions where you can name a specific issue. Re-read the requested quantity in math and verify the evidence in reading. Change an answer when you have a reason, rather than because it feels unfamiliar.

LET’S WORK THROUGH IT

Original worked example

You have spent a while on a math question and keep repeating the same calculation. What should you do?

Write down what the question asks, eliminate any impossible answers, choose your best remaining answer, and mark it. Move on and return within the module if time remains.

A tip worth keeping

Build your pacing plan from your own timed practice. A universal seconds-per-question rule will not fit every problem.

Your quick checklist

  • Identify the exact task before working.
  • Show the reasoning, not just the answer.
  • Review the mistake and try a new example.
