Build Your SAT Practice Routine

Turn practice into progress with a realistic weekly routine and an error log.

Start with a baseline

Use official practice to identify the types of questions that need attention. A baseline is information, not a judgment. Record whether each missed question came from a knowledge gap, a misread, or a pacing decision.

Choose one skill at a time

Spend a short session reviewing one relationship or rule, then apply it to several problems. Explain why each answer works. Mixing every topic immediately can make it hard to notice which skill is improving.

Review mistakes before adding volume

Rework a missed problem without looking at the solution. If you still cannot solve it, study the explanation, close it, and try again. Revisit the idea later with a different example to test understanding rather than memory.

Build a sustainable schedule

Use the sample below as a starting point. Adjust for your classes, activities, and test date. Short sessions that you can repeat are more useful than a plan you abandon after two days.

LET’S WORK THROUGH IT

Original worked example

Your error log shows three mistakes involving percent change. What should your next session focus on?

Review why the original value belongs in the denominator. Solve a price increase and a price decrease, then explain the setup aloud. Revisit the skill later with a new problem.

A tip worth keeping

Count reviewed mistakes and skills understood, not just hours spent at your desk.

Your quick checklist

  • Identify the exact task before working.
  • Show the reasoning, not just the answer.
  • Review the mistake and try a new example.
PUT IT INTO PRACTICE

Try it yourself

These original practice questions were written for this guide. Try each one before opening its explanation.

1. A student repeatedly misses negative signs. What should they record?

Show answer & explanation

The exact step where the sign changed and a checking habit, such as substituting the result into the original equation.

2. Why try a new question after reviewing an old one?

Show answer & explanation

A new example tests whether you understand the skill rather than remember the answer.

3. What should you change if your schedule is impossible to maintain?

Show answer & explanation

Reduce the session length or frequency while keeping focused practice and review.
