Turn words into quantities

Assign variables with clear meanings and units. For a rate problem, distance = rate × time, but the units must agree. Convert minutes to hours before multiplying by a rate measured in miles per hour.

Connect algebra and graphs

A linear equation describes a constant rate of change. A system’s solution satisfies both equations; on a graph it is an intersection. You can substitute an answer back into the original relationships to check it.

Use geometry labels carefully

In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is opposite the right angle. For sine, cosine, and tangent, opposite and adjacent depend on the reference angle. A side does not have one permanent label independent of the angle.

Estimate before calculating

Estimate the size and sign of your answer. If a rectangle’s length doubles while its width stays fixed, the area doubles. A calculator can evaluate arithmetic, but it cannot decide whether your setup models the question.

LET’S WORK THROUGH IT

Original worked example

A cyclist travels at 12 miles per hour for 35 minutes. How far does the cyclist travel?

Convert time: 35/60 = 7/12 hour. Distance = 12 × 7/12 = 7 miles. Multiplying 12 by 35 would incorrectly mix hours and minutes.

PUT IT INTO PRACTICE

Try it yourself

These original practice questions were written for this guide. Try each one before opening its explanation.

1. A right triangle has legs 9 and 12. Find the hypotenuse.

Show answer & explanation

15. Its square is 9² + 12² = 225.

2. A triangle has base 14 and height 5. Find its area.

Show answer & explanation

35 square units: 14 × 5 ÷ 2.

3. The opposite side is 6 and the hypotenuse is 10. Find sin θ.

Show answer & explanation

3/5, or 0.6. Sine is opposite divided by hypotenuse.
