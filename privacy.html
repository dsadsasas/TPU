What this site collects

You can read guides and download PDFs without creating an account. This site has no student database, payment system, or contact form. Its hosting provider may process basic request information, such as IP addresses, in its service logs.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is disabled in this sample until the site owner adds a real Measurement ID. Once configured, we ask before loading it. If you accept, Google Analytics 4 measures page views, approximate visitor counts, guide visits, and PDF download clicks. Events include the guide name, test type, and category. We do not send search terms or form content.

A download click is counted when a PDF link is clicked; it does not confirm that the file finished downloading.

Your choice

You can accept or decline optional analytics below. The choice is saved in your browser’s local storage when available. Declining leaves all guides and downloads available. Changing to decline reloads the page to stop further analytics on this page; previously collected data is not erased by that action.

Google’s processing

If you accept analytics, Google may use cookies and process technical information about your device and visit. See how Google uses information from partner sites for details.

Questions and updates

This notice should be reviewed by the site owner when analytics, hosting, or contact information changes. Visit our contact page for contact information.
